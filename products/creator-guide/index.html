01

Start with what you already have

A phone, a useful question and one clear demonstration are enough for a first experiment. The guide is for beginners, freelancers and small businesses.

02

Replace confusion with a process

Choose the problem, plan a few shots, film a test, edit for understanding and publish one useful next step.

03

Your first studio can be simple

A window, stable phone support and a quiet corner come before decorative lighting. Compare a test recording before buying.

04

Buy for the bottleneck

Illustrative budgets: €0–€20 using existing gear; €40–€80 prioritizing support and audio; €100–€180 considering one diffused light. These are targets, not current quotations.

05

Light is more useful than an effect

LED is a source; a softbox softens light; RGB controls color; a spotlight directs a beam; a GOBO projects a pattern. Start with a neutral key light.

06

Film a clear vertical story

Use 9:16 framing, check the horizon and microphone, then record an opening, demonstration and next step as separate clips.

07

Show a product honestly

Show scale, texture and use. The comparison below is an original schematic, not a claimed photographic result. Real colors and portions must stay truthful.

08

A hook makes a testable promise

Weak: “The best video trick ever.” Clearer: “Your horizon looks tilted? Check the grid and level.” The guide includes 50 original openings.

09

AI assists; you verify

Fifteen tool profiles cover writing, visuals, editing, audio and automation. Official access information was checked on 26 October 2026. Limits and rights can change.

10

Free does not mean unlimited

ChatGPT offers a limited free plan. Runway’s free credits are one-time. ElevenLabs Free does not include commercial rights. Always check the plan and license.

11

A prompt needs a real brief

“Using only [approved facts], write a 20-second video for [audience]. Give three shots, one CTA and a list of missing information.” Thirty adaptable prompts are included.

12

Edit for understanding

Remove repetition, correct captions, keep speech clear and license your music. A useful cut explains the action rather than adding movement for its own sake.

13

Thirty days, one project

The plan separates research, filming, editing, publishing and review. It is a practice sequence, not a daily-posting rule or a growth guarantee.
